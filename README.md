### 🚪 TwoDoor: a job-search decision app for macOS

**The problem:** job sites send many alert emails every week. People skim them, save some in a spreadsheet, and never actually decide about most roles.

**What it does:** every morning it gives you a short list of 2–8 roles and asks you to decide: apply, skip, or later. Before that, it reads your job-alert emails, finds the full job description, and checks each role against your own rules.

**The idea behind it:** every job has two separate questions:
- **Will they let me in?** Is the level right, and does my experience match the work?
- **Do I want to walk in?** Will I do good work there, and does the pay meet my floor?

The app always shows both answers side by side. It never shows a "87% match" score, because a score hides the reason.

**Built with:** Swift, SwiftUI, SQLite, Gmail (read-only), Gemini.

### 🌿 Calm Day Planner: a calm, visual daily planner

**The problem:** most planners are long text lists. For people who are easily distracted, a list is hard to start and easy to ignore.

**What it does:** it shows your day as a visual timeline, with timers and repeating tasks. It works offline, and your data stays on your device. Phone and laptop sync with end-to-end encryption, so the server cannot read your plans.

**How I ship it:** every change goes through three copies (staging, pre-prod, production) before users see it.

**Built with:** TypeScript, React, Vite, PWA, Cloudflare Pages and D1.
